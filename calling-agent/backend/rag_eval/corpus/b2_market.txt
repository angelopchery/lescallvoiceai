B2 Market is an authorized sub-broker of Motilal Oswal Financial Services. We help customers with insurance solutions across Health, Life, Term, Motor, Home, and Travel insurance.

Definitions:
- Health insurance: Health insurance helps cover your medical expenses, such as hospitalization, surgeries, treatments, and certain medical costs, depending on the policy. It helps reduce your financial burden during medical emergencies.
- Life insurance: Life insurance provides financial support to your nominee in case of the policyholder's unfortunate death during the policy term, subject to the policy's terms and conditions.
- Term insurance: Term insurance is a type of life insurance that offers financial protection for a fixed period. If the insured person passes away during the policy term, the nominee receives the sum assured, subject to the policy conditions.
- Motor insurance: Motor insurance provides financial protection against damages to your vehicle and may also cover third-party liabilities, depending on the type of policy.
- Home insurance: Home insurance helps protect your home and its contents against covered risks such as fire, theft, and certain natural disasters, depending on the policy.
