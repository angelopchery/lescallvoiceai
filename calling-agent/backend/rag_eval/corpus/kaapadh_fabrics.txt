Kaapadh Fabrics — KNOWLEDGE BASE

1. Polyester Knitted Fabrics
   High-performance polyester knitted fabrics engineered for durability, comfort, and large-scale garment production. Suitable for fashion apparel, sportswear, uniforms, and activewear applications requiring consistent quality and color retention.

2. Sportswear & Activewear Fabrics
   Lightweight, breathable, and moisture-friendly fabrics designed for sportswear, athleisure, gym wear, and performance apparel. Developed to provide comfort, flexibility, and long-lasting performance during intensive use

3. Digital Printed Fabrics
   Premium polyester fabrics enhanced with high-definition digital printing technology. Ideal for fashion brands seeking vibrant colors, detailed patterns, faster design development, and repeatable print consistency across production volumes.

4. Poly Spandex / Lycra Fabrics
   Stretchable knitted fabrics combining polyester durability with spandex flexibility. Commonly used in activewear, leggings, sportswear, and fitted garments where comfort, movement, and shape retention are essential.

5. Custom Knitted Fabric Development
   End-to-end fabric development tailored to specific brand requirements. Includes GSM customization, yarn selection, color matching, texture development, and performance enhancements to create fabrics optimized for your application and production needs.
